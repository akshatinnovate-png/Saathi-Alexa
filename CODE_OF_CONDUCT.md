# Code of Conduct

Saathi is built for elders and the people who love them. We want working on it to feel the same: kind, patient and safe.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text applies to every space the project runs: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any chat or call about Saathi.

## In short

- Be welcoming to people of every age, background, ability, language and level of experience.
- Assume good intent, explain instead of blaming, and accept feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, personal or political attacks, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Never share a real elder's or family's data in an issue or pull request. Use the fictional demo family (`npm run demo`).

## Reporting

Email **akshat.innovate@gmail.com** with the subject `Saathi conduct`. Reports are read by the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) and kept confidential. If a report is about a maintainer, that person takes no part in handling it. You will get a reply within 7 days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, then a permanent ban, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
