# Sahay for Android: origin and permission

Sahay for Android is based on **Senku** by Steve Aldrin and contributors, offered under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 (see `LICENSE.md` and `NOTICE`, kept unchanged).

Akshat Sarkar obtained permission from the author to remove Senku's charges and integrate it into Saathi. This copy:

- removes every plan, checkout, usage limit and update check, and the hosted sign-in;
- replaces Senku's hosted backend with direct calls to Google Gemini using the user's own free API key, stored encrypted with the Android Keystore;
- is distributed free of charge as part of Saathi, for noncommercial use.
